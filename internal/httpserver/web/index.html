What this does

Two things: shorten a URL into a memorable slug, or store a piece of text and get a link back. Both can expire on a schedule you choose, or last forever.

  • Create a redirect -- pick a slug, give it a target URL.
  • Create a paste -- paste text, pick a language for highlighting, get a link.
